## Bologna TMD (US-TMD / US-TM2017)

- **What:** raw smartphone sensor events, 13 users, 5 classes (car, bus, train, still, walking), ≤ 20 Hz
- **Source:** https://tempesta.cs.unibo.it/projects/us-tm2017/download.html
  (raw data: `static/dataset/raw_data/raw_data.tar.gz`)
- **Downloaded:** 2026-10-XX
- **Licence:** No explicit licence found on the download page or tutorial (checked 2026-10-XX).
  The site asks users to cite the publication below.
- **Citation:** Carpineti C., Lomonaco V., Bedogni L., Di Felice M., Bononi L.,
  "Custom Dual Transportation Mode Detection by Smartphone Devices Exploiting Sensor Diversity",
  Proc. 14th Workshop on Context and Activity Modeling and Recognition (IEEE COMOREA 2018),
  doi:10.1109/PERCOMW.2018.8480119
- **Use in this project:** exploration (block 1).
