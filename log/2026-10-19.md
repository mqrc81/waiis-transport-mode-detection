## Findings of LHS paper

* Results dropped from validation to test, because of:
    * a) Overfitting
    * b) Leaky cross-validation: Training frames came shuffled => randomly splitting into folds leads to near-twins
      in both training & validation, whereas test used new, unseen data
    * c) Different test data: New test hand position in 2019, and new test users in 2020
* Orientation handling: 2020 winner transformed coordinates using gravity sensor to express readings relative to
  person, not phone
* Bus vs car: Both show similar motion patterns and are difficult to discern. Stop-lights can additionally lead to
  false "still" readings.

## Comparing models & datasets

### UniMTS
* Pre-trained on HumanML3D dataset
  * simulated joint movements generated from text
  * 29h total
  * daily activities like walking, sports, dancing; no focus on vehicles
* Zero-shot based on custom classes
* Few-shot with k=1..10 examples per class
* Input:
  * `(samples, length, 6 × joints)`, with acc x,y,z then gyro x,y,z for each joint
* 10s window
