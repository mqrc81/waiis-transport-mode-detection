## Experiment 03: label curve on SHL User 1 (closed at stop-point 2)

*(Details in `experiments/03_label-curve_shl/README.md`; runs in MLflow `03_label-curve_shl`.)*

**Setup**

- SHL User 1, hips only; new day split 44 / 15 / 15 days (training / validation / test, ~60 / 20 / 20 % of hours
  per class), `splits/shl_u1_days.csv`.
- LightGBM on k windows per class, k ∈ {1, 2, 5, 10, 50, 200, 1000, all}, sampled round-robin over days and trips
  (`splits/samples_k.csv`, nested, seeds 0–4).
- Small grid per k on the validation days; k = all with 02's parameters. acc + gyro (145 features) vs acc only (116).

**Validation macro-F1, seed 0 only** (test days never evaluated)

| k    | acc + gyro | acc only |
|------|------------|----------|
| 10   | 0.559      | 0.570    |
| 200  | 0.698      | 0.690    |
| 1000 | 0.735      | 0.725    |
| all  | 0.779      | 0.769    |

- No flattening by k = 1000 (gap 0.044 to k = all).
- Still / walk / run / bike near saturation by k = 200; the remaining gain is in car, bus, train and subway.
- Gyroscope about + 0.01 at k ≥ 50, one seed only.

**Stopped** after the direction change (draft DECISIONS entry 2026-10-28): accelerometer only, final test = the 12
ExtraSensory test users, label curve to be redone on unseen ES users. Reusable: the split, `samples_k.csv`,
`tmd.sampling`.
