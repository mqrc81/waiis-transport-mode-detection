## Quality-Checking Bologna TMD Dataset

**Ran:** reader `src/tmd/bologna.py` + checks on all 16 users.

**Findings**

- 16 users; 13 match the paper's totals (±0.6%), 3 are small later additions.
- Units m/s² and rad/s, timestamps in ms. 4 corrupted files decoded OK.
- 12 users have acc + gyro, 4 accelerometer only.
- "Max 20 Hz" is a ceiling: recorded on change. Many users at 1–4 Hz, too sparse
  for gait; 2 users at ~200 Hz; only a few near 20 Hz.

**Proposed**

- Two tracks: 6-channel and accelerometer-only.
- Downsample > 20 Hz; never upsample sparse data (window needs ≥ 10 Hz real rate,
  no gap > 0.5 s).

**Expected (estimate, to confirm):** 6-channel ~2–3 users pass → no real
leave-one-user-out; acc-only ~8 users → Bologna mainly an accelerometer-only
cross-user test.

**Estimate of usable data:**

* 20Hz or more
* 6 channels (acc + gyro)

About **3 hours**, and nearly all of it from one user.

## Quality-Checking ExtraSensory Dataset

*(Done 2026-10-24 to 26 in experiment 02 step 1; details in `experiments/02_family-baseline_shl-es/README.md`,
tables in `results/audit/`.)*

**What's there.**
- 60 users: 34 iPhone, 26 Android (platform from `cv_5_folds`).
- 377,346 minutes in the cleaned CSVs. Each minute has one raw snippet: `raw_acc`, `proc_gyro`, `raw_magnet`.
- The original (uncleaned) labels were missing from the data root. I downloaded
  `ExtraSensory.per_uuid_original_labels.zip` (sha256 `7c017f75…7e79`) into `per_uuid_original_labels/`.
- The local unzip left the cleaned CSVs as one folder per file.

**Rates and units: not the documented "40 Hz, ~20 s".** Every acc snippet has 800 samples.

| | acc rate | snippet length | acc units | gyro rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| iPhone | ≈ 34 Hz | ≈ 23 s | g | ≈ 40 Hz |
| Android | mostly ≈ 50 Hz (some ≈ 100 Hz) | ≈ 16 s (8 s) | m/s² | 40–100 Hz |

- One Android user (BEF6C611) is in g for half of their minutes, so units are decided per snippet (median |acc| < 3 → g).
- Gyro is rad/s on both platforms.

**Missing and broken data.**
- No gyroscope: 61359772, CCAF77F0 and F50235E0 (all Android).
- 617 other minutes have no gyro file, and 290 minutes have no acc file.
- About 1.3k acc snippets and 1.2k gyro snippets (mostly Android, 33 users) have time running backwards or a span
  over 40 s. They are dropped and counted.

**Labels.**
- `ON_A_TRAIN`: 344 minutes from 5 users, present only in the original labels. On 342 of them the cleaned CSV says still.
- `ON_A_BUS` + `I'M_A_PASSENGER` (475 minutes) now counts as bus instead of a bus + car conflict.
- Motorbike: 86 minutes from one user. Kept out of training as a separate check.
- Plane, boat, skateboard, stationary bike, treadmill, elliptical, wheelchair and elevator minutes are dropped.
- Still was 31% sleeping (phone on a nightstand), and in 44% of still minutes the phone wasn't carried. Rules v2:
  - drop sleeping;
  - drop walk / run / bike minutes where the phone isn't carried;
  - cap not-carried still at 20% per user.
- 41–47% of minutes have no phone position reported.

**Splits.** 12 test users (7 iPhone, 5 Android). The 48 dev users are in 5 folds by user. Written to `splits/es_users.csv`.
